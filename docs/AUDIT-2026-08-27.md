## 1. The incident

| Probe | taniniaga.shop | zvara.shop | permatamall.shop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/locations?search=medan&level=district` | 500 | 500 | 500 |
| `POST /hello` (login, wrong password) | — | **500** | — |
| `GET /` | 200 | 200 | 200 |

Both reports were one bug, and it was fleet-wide. `wrangler tail zvarashop`
during a login POST logged `admin-login Error: KV put() limit exceeded for the
day`. The locations route logged nothing: its `catch` returned a generic 500
without a `console.error`, which is why the outage could not be diagnosed from
Workers Logs alone — fixed as `public-location-search-failed`.

**Root cause.** Workers KV on the Free plan allows 1,000 writes per day for the
whole account; `wrangler kv namespace list` shows 21 namespaces on this one,
belonging to every product the operator runs. `checkRateLimit` wrote one KV
counter per public request — including the kecamatan search the checkout form
fires on every keystroke after a 100 ms debounce — and the login wrote the
session record plus three failure counters. Once the pool ran dry, each of those
writes threw; the search route swallowed it into a 500 and the login route
reported a system error.

**Why it was structural, not a spike.** Every install shares the pool with
every other Worker on the account, so no single store's traffic explains or
controls it. A store of record behind a shared daily quota is the defect;
the fix is ADR-021 (sessions and counters in D1, KV cache-only, no counter on
a free computation). Whether the account also moves to Workers Paid is an
operational decision the code no longer depends on.

## 2. AutoLaris QRIS / VA on the hybrid form — trace

Happy path, verified against the tree: `/api/payment-methods` lists `cod`,
then `QRIS` and the VA channels from `AUTOLARIS_CHANNEL_OPTIONS`, then manual
bank accounts → the form posts `payment_method` + `payment_channel` to
`/api/submit-order` → `persistOrder` (`payment_status = 'pending'`) →
`createAutoLarisPaymentForOrder` inserts a `payment_transactions` row, calls
`POST /api/h2h/create_payment`, stores `virtual_account` / `qr_payload` /
provider totals → the response carries them to the browser, which stores
`thanks_state` and navigates to `/payment` → `/payment` renders the QR or VA and
polls `POST /api/order-status` every minute until `is_paid`, which today only a
manual operator reconciliation sets (UNIMPLEMENTED_SPECS §AutoLaris).

Findings, in severity order. Fixed ones name their evidence.

| # | Finding | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A single `create_payment` failure marked the **order** `failed` — releasing its reserved stock — and the unique index on `payment_transactions.order_id` plus `if (existing) return existing` meant no second attempt was possible. The buyer landed on `/payment` with a "hubungi Admin" notice and nothing else | **Fixed.** Order stays `pending`; a `failed`/`expired` row is reused for a retry; `/api/order-status` regenerates on `retry_payment: true`; `/payment` offers the button. `autolaris-payment.test.ts` |
| 2 | `expires_at` was written and rendered as a countdown but nothing ever set `status = 'expired'`; the `expired` branches in reconciliation and both public pages were unreachable, and `/payment` polled a dead VA forever | **Fixed.** `effectivePaymentStatus` derives `expired` at read time for every reader; `/payment` stops polling a dead instruction |
| 3 | `/api/order-status` had no rate limit — unauthenticated, one D1 read per call, polled by every open tab | **Fixed.** 60/min per address; retries 5 per 10 min. Observed locally: 65 calls → 60 × 404, 5 × 429 |
| 4 | Two concurrent submits for one order raced on the unique index; the loser's exception marked the order `failed` | **Fixed.** The insert is caught and the surviving row returned |
| 5 | `expectedAdminFee` / `expectedBilledTotal` were computed and never compared with the provider's figures, so a provider fee change silently rebilled the buyer a total never shown at checkout | **Partly.** The provider figure is still what is stored (it is what will be charged); a mismatch now logs `autolaris-fee-mismatch` so the fee table gets corrected |
| 6 | `callbackUrl` still points at `/api/webhooks/autolaris`, a `410` tombstone | **Fixed in 1.3.3 (A-164, ADR-022).** The endpoint now records every delivery in `autolaris_callbacks` and answers 200; it still moves no payment state. | 7 | `/payment` depends entirely on `sessionStorage`; a second tab or device cannot reach a VA that is in D1 | **Fixed in 1.3.3 (A-165).** Checkout navigates to `/payment#o=…&t=…`; the page reads the fragment, stores it, and strips it; a *Salin Tautan Pembayaran* button reproduces the link. ## 3. What the audit found healthy

- The location search design is already right in shape: keystroke suggestions
  come from the bundled district catalogue and only a selection reaches
  Mengantar. The outage was the counter around it, not the search.
- Authorisation is enforced in middleware from Astro's normalized URL, and the
  auth test suite asserts security properties rather than happy paths; the
  session store could be swapped underneath it without touching a route.
- Manual reconciliation is properly idempotent (unique audit index, append-only
  triggers), in contrast to the create path it now matches.
